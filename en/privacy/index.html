Privacy policy

What data this website and the application process — and on what legal basis.

Draft. This text is a template and has not been reviewed by a lawyer — have it checked before publishing.
Last updated: 2026-09-09

1. Controller

The controller for data processing on this website is the entity named in the imprint. For privacy matters you can reach us at the address given on the contact page.

2. When you visit this website

When you open these pages your browser transmits technically necessary data to the hosting provider, which is stored in server log files: IP address, date and time, page requested, referrer, and browser and operating system identifiers. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure, uninterrupted operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Google Fonts

These pages load typefaces from Google servers (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), which transmits your IP address to Google. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a consistent presentation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Serving the fonts locally is being prepared.

4. Local storage in your browser

This website sets no tracking cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is your choice between the light and dark appearance (key planoramix-theme). That value never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time in your browser settings.

5. Account and project data in the application

Creating an account in PLANoramix Studio stores your email address and sign-in credentials, along with the project content you create while using it. This data is processed to perform the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Processors

To deliver the service we use carefully selected providers, each covered by a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase Inc. — Datenbank und Authentifizierung
  • Stripe, Inc., 510 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA — Zahlungsabwicklung
  • Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA — DNS und E-Mail-Weiterleitung

7. Retention

Log files are deleted after 30 days at the latest. Account and project data are kept for the duration of the contractual relationship and deleted afterwards, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

8. Your rights

  • Access to the data held about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Complaint to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

9. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our processing or the legal situation changes. The version published here is the one that applies.
