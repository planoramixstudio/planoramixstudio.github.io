Load the floor plan and set its scale

In the layout you work on the architect’s floor plan. Load it as a DXF, PDF or image and give it the right scale, so lengths and areas are true.

Updated 27 October 2026 · App version 494813a

my.planoramix.studio
Step 1 of 5
Mouse wheel or ← → to change step

More details

  • The longer the distance, the more accurate the scale: use an overall length or a scale bar, not a door width.
  • If a distance is wrong, just tap two new points.
  • With a DXF, Detect rooms finds the closed rooms automatically.
